Privacy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Download analytics

When you download Sonexis, we record aggregate statistics including approximate country, region, city, continent, time zone, browser family, device type, preferred language, referring website, app version, and download timestamp. Approximate location comes from your network address and may be inaccurate. The most recent 2,000 download events are retained in an access-restricted event log.

Website visits

Each homepage load, including refreshes, records an aggregate website-visit count by date and source. Links containing a campaign tag, such as ?ref=resume, identify that source. We keep the tag in your browser's session storage to associate later visits and download-button clicks in the same tab with that source. Traffic without a current or remembered tag is counted as untagged. Closing the tab ends this storage, and a new tagged link replaces the previous tag. The tag is also stored with recent visit and download events. We retain the most recent 2,000 visit events in an access-restricted log, including timestamp, tag, approximate location, browser family, device type, language, referring hostname, and a shortened secret-keyed visitor fingerprint. Referring URL paths and query strings are not stored. These statistics measure visits and button clicks, not completed downloads, installations, or activity inside the app.

Visitor fingerprints and unique download estimates

We use your network address and browser identifier briefly on the server to generate a secret-keyed fingerprint for an aggregate, approximate unique-download count. A shortened version of that fingerprint is stored with recent visit and download events so repeated activity can be recognized approximately. We do not store the raw network address or full browser identifier, and the fingerprint cannot be reversed to retrieve them.

Access and purpose

These statistics help us understand Sonexis adoption and prioritize compatibility work. Detailed analytics are restricted to the Sonexis developer and are not sold or used for advertising.
