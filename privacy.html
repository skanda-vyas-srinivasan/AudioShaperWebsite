Privacy

Last updated September 10, 2026

Download analytics

When you download Sonexis, we record aggregate statistics including approximate country, region, city, continent, time zone, browser family, device type, preferred language, referring website, app version, and download date. Approximate location comes from your network address and may be inaccurate.

Unique download estimates

We use your network address and browser identifier briefly on the server to generate a secret-keyed fingerprint for an aggregate, approximate unique-download count. We do not store the raw network address or an individual download history, and the aggregate counter cannot be used to retrieve individual fingerprints.

Access and purpose

These statistics help us understand Sonexis adoption and prioritize compatibility work. Detailed analytics are restricted to the Sonexis developer and are not sold or used for advertising.
